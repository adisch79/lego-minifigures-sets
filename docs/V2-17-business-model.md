# V2-17 Business model: options paper

Date 2026-11-16. Facts and sources: [research/V2-17-business-model-research.md](research/V2-17-business-model-research.md). Not legal advice. Owner decides.

## Starting point
- Free fan site, one owner, hosting on free tiers (Cloudflare Pages, Supabase). Running cost today is close to zero, so any income is a bonus, not survival.
- Biggest constraint: figure and card images come from third parties (BrickLink, Rebrickable, LEGO). LEGO Fair Play allows the LEGO name only "in a non-commercial manner" and says nothing about ads or affiliate links. Brickset and Rebrickable run affiliate links openly.
- Traffic is unknown, so no income figure in this paper is sourced. Every band below is UNKNOWN until we have visitor numbers.

## Options

| # | Option | What the visitor sees | Effort | Cost to owner | Legal risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Donations | "Buy me a coffee" page (V2-15, already in build) | S | Ko-fi tips 0% + processor fee; Buy Me a Coffee 5% + Stripe 2.9% + $0.30 | Low |
| 2 | Affiliate links | "Buy" links on series and figure pages to Amazon (Toys 3%), LEGO.com (Rakuten, rate unpublished), eBay (rate needs login) | S-M | None; disclosure line required | Low-med (LEGO terms seen only after approval) |
| 3 | Ads (AdSense) | Banner ads | M (EU needs a certified consent banner) | Slower pages, consent banner | Med (ads on third-party images, not covered by Fair Play) |
| 4 | Premium tier | Paid extras (e.g. backups, exports), like Rebrickable Pro $49/year | L (billing, support, tax) | Payment provider fees, support time | Med-high (charging money for a product built on third-party images) |

Not possible: EthicalAds and Carbon (developer sites only).

## Recommendation
1. **Now: donations (option 1).** The page is being built in V2-15. Use **Ko-fi** instead of Buy Me a Coffee: 0% platform fee on one-time tips vs 5%. Page copy stays "Buy me a coffee".
2. **Next: affiliate links (option 2), one shop first.** Start with Amazon Associates (published 3% rate, simple sign-up). Apply for the LEGO.com program in parallel and add it only after its terms are read. Show the disclosure line on every page with links. Measure clicks for 2-3 months before adding more shops.
3. **Not now: ads and premium (options 3 and 4).** Worst income per effort and the highest image-rights risk. Revisit only if traffic is large.

## Owner decisions needed
- A. Approve the plan (donations now, then affiliate links), or pick another option.
- B. Donation platform: Ko-fi (recommended) or Buy Me a Coffee. You create the account; the link goes into the V2-15 page.
- C. Affiliate: OK to sign up for Amazon Associates (and apply to LEGO.com via Rakuten) in your name? Both need an adult account holder and tax details.
- D. Optional: a short written question to LEGO (fan site with affiliate links, OK?). It removes the biggest UNKNOWN.

## Next steps after the decision
Affiliate links become a new backlog story (link placement mockups, disclosure line, click count). Gaps to close then: LEGO affiliate terms, eBay toys rate, image licences for Rebrickable and BrickLink.
