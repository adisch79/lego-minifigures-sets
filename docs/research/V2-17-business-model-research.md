# V2-17 Business model research (Minifig Hunt)

Researched 2026-11-16. Rule: every number carries a source; not found = UNKNOWN. Several primary pages were blocked (403/402/login), so some facts come from search snippets; marked (secondary) where weak. Not legal advice.

## 1. Affiliate
- LEGO.com program: sign-up via LinkShare (Rakuten); age 18+; online form; commission on validated checkout sales excluding delivery, taxes, returns; monthly reporting. SOURCE: https://lego.com/page/affiliate-program
- LEGO commission % and cookie days: UNKNOWN (not published on LEGO page). Third parties say ~3% (range 2.4-5%) and 7 or 30 days, conflicting, unverified. SOURCE (secondary): https://marketplace.referly.so/affiliate-programs/lego , https://strackr.com/blog/lego-affiliate-program
- LEGO trademark-bidding ban: UNKNOWN (terms only visible after approval). Pattern in other Awin toy-retail programs: no bidding on the brand name. Example (Minifigure Store, not LEGO): https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/114518
- Peer proof: Brickset is a LEGO affiliate; active merchants listed: LEGO, Amazon, eBay, Brick Owl, Rebrickable, Catawiki and others; BrickLink listed as not actively promoted (Mar 2026). Typical rate "a few percent". SOURCE: https://brickset.com/article/52661/affiliate-marketing-and-sponsored-article-disclosure
- Amazon Associates: Toys 3.00% fixed standard rate (US). SOURCE: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GRXPHT8U84RAYDXZ?lang=en
- Amazon cookie 24 h, cart extension 90 days: (secondary) https://getlasso.co/amazon-affiliate-commission-rate/
- Amazon rules: must show "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases"; must use tagged link formats; no public statements about the program without permission. SOURCE: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/agreement . Other policy items (180-day qualifying-sales rule, cloaking, price display): UNKNOWN, in Program Policies, not fetched.
- eBay Partner Network: pays % of sale by region and sub-category; Toys & Hobbies exact %: UNKNOWN (rate card needs login). Third parties say 1-4% overall (secondary). SOURCE: https://partnernetwork.ebay.com.au/resources/our-new-partner-platform , https://strackr.com/blog/ebay-affiliate-program
- BrickLink: no affiliate program found. UNKNOWN. SOURCE (nothing official found): https://brickset.com/article/52661/affiliate-marketing-and-sponsored-article-disclosure
- Brick Owl: sellers pay 2.5% store commission; optional affiliate schemes exist in store settings, rates UNKNOWN. SOURCE: https://www.brickowl.com/help/store-fees

## 2. Donations (platform fees, before tax)
- Buy Me a Coffee: 5% platform fee, no monthly fee; Stripe 2.9% + $0.30; payout 0.5%; +1% non-US; +0.5% subscriptions. SOURCE: https://help.buymeacoffee.com/en/articles/8105744
- Ko-fi free plan: 0% on one-time tips; 5% on shop, memberships, commissions; Gold $12/month removes the 5%; PayPal/Stripe processor fees apply on top. SOURCE (search snippets of Ko-fi pages): https://ko-fi.com/pricing , https://help.ko-fi.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002506494-Does-Ko-fi-take-a-fee
- GitHub Sponsors: personal-account sponsors pay no fee, developer gets 100%; organization sponsors up to 6% (3% card + 3% GitHub). Developer-oriented, weak fit for a fan audience. SOURCE: https://docs.github.com/en/billing/concepts/third-party-payments/github-sponsors
- PayPal donations (US personal account): 2.89% + $0.49 domestic, +1.50% international. Varies by country and account type; owner country UNKNOWN. SOURCE (search snippet): https://www.paypal.com/us/digital-wallet/paypal-consumer-fees

## 3. Ads
- AdSense: no traffic minimum stated; needs original content, age 18+, policy compliance, HTML access. SOURCE: https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9724
- AdSense in EEA/UK/CH: a Google-certified CMP integrated with IAB TCF is required to serve personalized ads (EEA/UK since 16 Jan 2024, CH since 31 Jul 2024). Without it only non-personalized/limited ads. SOURCE: https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/13554116
- AdSense RPM for toy/hobby niche: no authoritative figure. Blog estimates: entertainment/gaming $1-$5 or $2-$8; new blogs $1-$3; US gaming traffic $5-$12. Low confidence. SOURCE (secondary): https://www.monetizemore.com/blog/website-ad-revenue , https://ranktracker.com/blog/adsense-revenue-by-niche-how-much-can-you-earn
- EthicalAds: developer-focused sites only, 50k+ monthly pageviews, about $2.50 CPM, $50 minimum payout, no tracking. A LEGO fan site does not qualify. SOURCE: https://www.ethicalads.io/publishers/
- Carbon Ads: audience is developer/design/AI sites (350+ curated); no publisher minimum or revenue share published. Not a fit. SOURCE: https://www.carbonads.net/
- Peers: Brickset shows no ads to logged-in free members and asks them to use affiliate links; BrickEconomy gives registered users a mostly ad-free experience. SOURCE: https://brickset.com/article/110571 , https://www.brickeconomy.com/support-us
- Rebrickable dropped third-party ads in 2024, keeps LEGO affiliate links, relies on Pro/Designer plans and MOC sales. SOURCE: https://rebrickable.com/blog/797/rebrickable-2024-stats-year-in-review/ , https://rebrickable.com/blog/777/admin-update/

## 5. Legal / ToS
- LEGO Fair Play: logo must never be used on unofficial sites; LEGO trademark may be used "in a non-commercial manner", as adjective, with registered mark symbol; no LEGO in domain name; disclaimer required; LEGO owns copyright in catalog and packaging photos and instructions, tolerates limited unaltered extracts for non-commercial use. Page does not mention ads or affiliate links: UNKNOWN whether a monetized fan site is tolerated (Brickset and others do it). SOURCE: https://www.lego.com/en-us/legal/notices-and-policies/fair-play/
- LEGO enforces the domain rule via WIPO. SOURCE: https://brickset.com/article/27731/lego-in-domain-names
- LEGO.com terms: copying site images for public or commercial purposes needs written permission from LEGO System A/S (secondary summary). SOURCE: https://lego.com/en-fi/legal/terms-of-use
- Rebrickable API: any purpose incl. commercial, free or paid apps; attribution requested, not required; MOC data excluded; scraping pages forbidden; catalog CSV downloads usable commercially with acknowledgement, max once a day. SOURCE: https://rebrickable.com/terms/ , https://rebrickable.com/downloads/
- Rebrickable image license: UNKNOWN (open forum question, ambiguous). SOURCE: https://forum.rebrickable.com/t/licenses-of-catalog-downloads/168980
- Brickset API v3: key required; new keys 100 getSets calls/day (secondary); commercial use and image rights UNKNOWN. SOURCE: https://brickset.com/article/52664/api-version-3-documentation
- BrickLink: ToS (18 Sep 2025) restricts reuse of designer models/images; catalog images are official LEGO or contributor photos; API and commercial image terms UNKNOWN. SOURCE: https://v2.bricklink.com/terms-of-service
